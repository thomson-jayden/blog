## Walking without a checklist

The best mornings started without a destination. Side streets opened into gardens, workshops, and tiny cafes that would never have made an itinerary.

## A different pace

Travel becomes more memorable when there is enough empty space to notice ordinary details. A train arriving, steam over a bowl of noodles, or bicycles lined up outside a school can say more about a place than a crowded landmark.
