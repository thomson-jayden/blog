Good interfaces make uncertainty feel manageable. In this post I share a few practical patterns for designing resilient states, from loading and errors to thoughtful empty states.

## Make progress visible

When a task takes time, users need a clear sense of what is happening. Showing progress, status, and meaningful feedback is often more valuable than adding another button.

## Design the failure state

Plan for the worst. A clear error state should tell people what happened, what they can do next, and how to recover.
